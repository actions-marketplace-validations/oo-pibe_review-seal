# Battlecard freshness check

You are checking a sales battlecard about a competitor. Reps read these on live calls, so a wrong fact costs us credibility.
Report a finding for each of these:

- A price, plan name, tier or free-tier claim about the competitor that their current pricing page or recent coverage contradicts.
- A feature claim ("they have no X", "their setup takes Y") that current sources contradict, or that you cannot find any source for.
- A "Last updated" date more than 30 days before today.
- A claim about our own product that is not in `data/approved-claims.md`. Read that file.

For each finding, give the competitor's current fact and where you found it, or say that you found no source.
Do not report tone or formatting.

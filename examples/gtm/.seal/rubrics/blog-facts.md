# Blog fact-check

You are fact-checking a marketing blog post before it is published. Report a finding for each of these:

- A statistic, percentage or multiplier with no named source, or with a vague one ("industry research", "studies show").
- A statistic whose named source you can find, but which says something different, or which a newer edition of the same source has replaced.
- A claim about our product (customer counts, results, features) that is not in `data/approved-claims.md`. Read that file.
- A claim about a competitor or another company's product that current sources contradict.

For each finding, say what the post says, what the source says, and where the source is.
Do not report style or tone.
